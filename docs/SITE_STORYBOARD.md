# Site storyboard

## Creative concept in ten points

1. The site opens beneath the interface, inside a calm working system.
2. An abstract route map establishes backend ownership before tools are named.
3. Every animated path represents understandable product behavior.
4. The map resolves into real project architecture as the page scrolls.
5. Evidence appears within narrative sentences, not detached statistic cards.
6. Dark technical chapters alternate with a warm editorial chapter.
7. Each major project receives a different architecture-based visual identity.
8. Signal colors communicate health, routing, and verified outcomes.
9. Motion reveals causality and disappears cleanly for reduced-motion users.
10. The final route ends in one proposition: bring Abdalrhman the complicated part.

## Chapter 1 — The invisible system

Immediate identity, positioning, headline, concise description, availability, location, social access, and two actions. The system map is the second voice of the hero, not an illustration beside it.

Primary action scrolls directly to selected systems. Secondary action reaches the closing conversation section.

## Chapter 2 — Proof, not claims

A single evidence rail scopes each fact to its source. Platform credentials, commerce scale, and education-platform reach remain visibly distinct. The first two viewport heights contain both positioning and proof.

## Chapter 3 — Selected systems

Four editorial case-study covers:

1. Merchant Operations and Salla Automation
2. Your Obour Guide
3. Smart Vending and Wasfaty Integration
4. AI PDF Extraction Pipeline

The homepage vertical slice fully art-directs the first cover. Later covers reuse the narrative framework, not the same visual template.

## Chapter 4 — The journey

A route grows in complexity across six moments: early curiosity, real client exposure, backend specialization, production integrations, complete product delivery, and system ownership. Education evidence supports the journey without becoming a résumé dump.

## Chapter 5 — Engineering range

Capabilities form connected layers: product backends, reliability and scale, data and integrations, product delivery, and intelligent systems. Visitors read the kinds of problems Abdalrhman can own before scanning technologies.

## Chapter 6 — Engineering principles

Six compact positions connect principle to practice. Examples include idempotent integration handling, visible long-running workflows, and deployment as part of delivery.

## Chapter 7 — Contact

The architecture route terminates in a direct invitation: “Bring me the part everyone calls complicated.” Contact methods remain standard links. No command palette or form is required for the first release.

## Planned supporting routes

- `/work`: featured systems and a compact supporting archive
- `/work/[slug]`: reusable case-study narrative
- `/about`: journey and working approach
- `/contact`: focused contact options
- `/design-system`: implemented visual QA route, absent from production navigation

## Vertical-slice boundary

The first implementation includes navigation, hero, accessible system visual, evidence rail, first project cover transition, mobile layout, reduced-motion support, and the closing contact route. Remaining chapters may follow once this visual language is validated.
